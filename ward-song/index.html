Farmington Bay 6th Ward

Our Ward Song

Farmington Bay Sixth Ward

Verse 1

We gather as neighbors, a ward of the Lord,
With hearts full of purpose and faith in His word.
In every small kindness, in meetings and prayer,
We remember the Savior—His love fills the air.

Verse 2

We walk in His footsteps, we try to be true,
To live as He lived and to do as He'd do.
Through trials and weakness, through joy and through tears,
He perfects us in mercy, He calms all our fears.

Chorus

We remember Him, in all that we do,
Following His ways, His light shining through.
Perfected in Christ as we strive to be whole,
Back to our Father through Jesus our goal.
Oh, how we love Him—with all of our heart—
Farmington Bay Sixth Ward, never apart.

Verse 3

We teach of His Atonement, we sing of His name,
We serve one another and honor His claim.
From little ones singing to parents who pray,
We keep Him the center of every new day.

Verse 4

He calls us to higher, to kinder, to more,
To lift up the weary and open the door.
In covenants kept and in charity shown,
We become more like Him—we're never alone.

Chorus

We remember Him, in all that we do,
Following His ways, His light shining through.
Perfected in Christ as we strive to be whole,
Back to our Father through Jesus our goal.
Oh, how we love Him—with all of our heart—
Farmington Bay Sixth Ward, never apart.

Bridge

He is the Way, He is the Light,
He is the Truth that makes all things right.
We love You, dear Savior, we'll follow You home,
Through You to the Father—we're never alone.

Final Chorus

We remember Him, in all that we do,
Following His ways, His light shining through.
Perfected in Christ as we strive to be whole,
Back to our Father through Jesus our goal.
Oh, how we love Him—with all of our heart—
Farmington Bay Sixth Ward, never apart.
Never apart… forever in Him.

Proposed 2027 Temple and Family History Plan

Based on Michael Burton’s proposal; edited draft for discussion.

One action for each month of 2027, as Michael Burton proposed it. This page puts that proposal on the Bay 6 Hub so members can read it and do it.

The twelve actions, scripture references, and numbers below are his. Schedule notes, helpful links, and the checklist were added for this draft and are marked so they are not read as part of the proposal.

Monthly Actions

Proposed 2027 monthly actions. Draft for Ward Council review. Not approved.
Month Focus Scripture Action Schedule note
January 5th Sunday Lesson ScriptureDoctrine & Covenants 128:15 “…they without us cannot be made perfect—neither can we without our dead be made perfect.” ActionShare stories and testimonies of the blessings of family history and temple work on both sides of the veil. Schedule noteProposed as a January 5th Sunday lesson. The date depends on Bishopric scheduling and is not set by this draft. Not part of the proposal text.
February Login & Recommend Check ScriptureMoroni 6:4–5 “Their names were taken, that they might be remembered and nourished…” ActionLog in to FamilySearch + schedule a temple recommend interview (if needed).
March Upload a Photo ScriptureMoses 6:46 “Enoch kept a book of remembrance.” ActionUpload 1 photo to FamilySearch.
April Find 1 Name ScriptureMalachi 4:5–6 Elijah restores the sealing keys. ActionAdd at least 1 new person to your family tree.
May Submit Names to the Temple Scripture1 Corinthians 15:29 Baptisms for the dead. ActionPrepare and bring at least 1 family name / ordinance card to the temple.
June 4 Generations Check ScriptureAbraham 1:31 Records of the fathers preserved. ActionVerify (and correct if needed) your first 4 generations in FamilySearch.
July Salt Lake Temple Open House ScriptureIsaiah 42:7 “To open the blind eyes…” ActionParticipate in one of the Salt Lake Temple open house events Schedule noteThe proposal does not list open-house dates. This draft has not confirmed them. Any dates depend on the official Church schedule. Not part of the proposal text.
August Upload a Story or Document ScriptureJohn 14:2 “In my Father’s house are many mansions.” ActionUpload 1 story or document to FamilySearch.
September Ward Temple Day ScriptureDoctrine & Covenants 109:22 “…go forth from this house armed with thy power…” ActionParticipate in the all-day ward temple session (ordinances for the living and the dead). Schedule noteThe proposal does not name a day. The date is still to be determined and is not placed on the ward calendar by this draft. Not part of the proposal text.
October Temple Preparation Focus ScriptureDoctrine & Covenants 138:57–58 “…continue their labors in the preaching of the gospel…” ActionComplete any remaining ordinance cards or help a family member get ready for the temple.
November Submit 5 Names ScriptureObadiah 1:21 “Saviours shall come up on mount Zion…” ActionPrint and submit at least 5 ordinance cards.
December Record a Family Tradition ScriptureRevelation 7:15 “…serve him day and night in his temple.” ActionRecord a family tradition in writing, photos, or audio and add it as a memory in FamilySearch.

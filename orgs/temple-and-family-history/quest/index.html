Interview a Grandparent

Ask a grandparent, or another older relative, these three questions. Listen. You do not have to write the answers here.

  1. What was your favorite thing to do when you were my age?
  2. Tell me a story about your family when you were young.
  3. What do you hope I will remember about our family?

Draw Your Family Tree

On paper, draw you at the bottom, your parents above you, and your grandparents above them. Write a name if you know it. Color it.

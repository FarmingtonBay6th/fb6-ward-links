How to join the Ward Calendar
  1. Open churchofjesuschrist.org/calendar and sign in with your Church Account.
  2. Tap the gear (Settings) icon, then Choose Calendars, and check the Farmington Bay 6th Ward and stake calendars.
  3. To add them to your phone, tap the gear icon, then Sync. Pick Apple, Google or Outlook and follow the steps shown to add your personal sync link. Keep that link to yourself.
  4. Or just view them: the Member Tools app shows ward and stake events (view only).

Updates can take a few minutes to a few hours to reach your phone. Church help: Using the Calendar

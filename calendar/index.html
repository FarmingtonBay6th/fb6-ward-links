Why Two Calendars?

Official Ward Calendar is the Church calendar. It's the source, with full details, but it needs a Church Account sign-in.

Bay 6 Calendar (this page) copies the public ward events (plus Farmington Bay Stake activities and Churchwide events) from the official calendar automatically every 8 hours, so you can open it without signing in. Private items like interviews are never copied.

Need to add or change an event? Make the change on the official ward calendar. It shows up here at the next sync.
